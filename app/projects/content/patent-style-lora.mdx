A style LoRA for Stable Diffusion XL that renders any object as a US patent-style
drawing: black lines on a white page with a single centered figure. Built as a
learning project to test whether a small adapter actually beats a well-written
prompt ("patent drawing, black and white line art").

## How it was built

Training data is 24 hand-picked figures from public-domain US design patents
(DeepPatent), cleaned, padded to 1024×1024 and captioned with a trigger word, view
and object only, with no style words. Training used the Diffusers SDXL LoRA script
(rank 16, learning rate 1e-4) on a Colab GPU. The first run, UNet only, failed with
coloured backgrounds and a weak trigger. The second added a noise offset and
text-encoder training, which fixed both. Checkpoint 1,500 of 3,000 was kept, since
later checkpoints overfit.

## Evaluation

Prompts and seeds were fixed and the baselines rendered before training: 10 subjects
(6 unseen) across 3 seeds. On unseen objects the LoRA produced a white page 99.8% of
the time versus 44.0% for the patent-drawing prompt and 0.1% for a plain prompt, with
gray shading cut from 74.6% to 3.8% and the CLIP subject score slightly higher
(0.316 vs 0.301). The metrics don't catch fake text, extra objects or impossible
parts, and the outputs are illustrations rather than valid patent drawings.

The adapter is published on Hugging Face (`Jatin1999/patent-style-lora`), and the
repo includes the Colab notebooks, configs, eval prompts and a Gradio demo app.
